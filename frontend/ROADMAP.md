# Roadmap

Figures queued for the sheet, in the order they were asked for. Each one had to
earn its place the same way the first two did: a real object, drawn part by part
from the kit, driven by one number, and interesting to watch for longer than a
glance.

## Built

All ten are on the sheet, and every one shipped with its own page and its own
registry item:

- the-zip
- the-turntable
- the-padlock
- the-safe
- the-escalator
- the-scanner
- the-safe-dial
- the-loom
- the-lighthouse
- the-claw-machine

The briefs that the last four were built from, kept because the reasoning still
holds:

**the-safe-dial.** A dial, not a door. The safe already covers the door swinging
open, so this one is the lock face on its own: one number is the dial's angle,
the three numbers land in order, and the bolt throws back only when the
combination is right. The interesting part is that it is the first figure the
reader can get *wrong*.

**the-loom.** Many threads into one cloth, which is what every integrations
section is trying to say. The heddle lifts every other warp thread, the shuttle
flies the weft across, the beater packs the row home, and the cloth grows toward
the reader. Four readings of one number.

**the-lighthouse.** A fixed point in the dark, which is what an uptime page is
selling. A tapering tower, a railed gallery, a glazed lamp room, and a beam that
sweeps the water. It has to stay calm while it moves, so nothing in it is quick.

**the-claw-machine.** The pick, out of a pile of near-identical things. The
gantry runs the rail, the claw drops on its cable, closes on one capsule, lifts
it clear and carries it to the chute - and the case stays glazed the whole way,
because a claw machine you can see inside of is not a claw machine.

## Queued

Nothing, for now. The next one gets picked when a page needs it.
